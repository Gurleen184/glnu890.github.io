04 / RESPONSIBLE DATA PRACTICE

Good analysis needs
good judgment.

Data and machine learning outputs are only useful when their limitations are understood. In my work, I aim to pay attention to data quality, explainability, privacy, and the context behind a result.

  • Check data for missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies.
  • Use appropriate evaluation measures and compare model performance.
  • Recognize that predictions are estimates, not guarantees.
  • Protect private or sensitive information and communicate limitations clearly.
